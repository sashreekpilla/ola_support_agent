# Ola Support Policy Handbook

## 1. Ticket Priority Classification
Tickets are prioritised by severity and customer impact. Sev-1 covers safety, widespread outage, or major service failure; Sev-2 covers significant single-customer disruption; Sev-3 covers routine issues and questions.

## 2. SLA by Severity
Sev-1 tickets should receive an initial response within 1 hour and receive immediate operational attention. Sev-2 tickets should receive an initial response within 4 hours, while Sev-3 tickets should receive an initial response within 1 business day.

## 3. Escalation Matrix
Sev-1 incidents are escalated immediately to the operations escalation desk. Sev-2 cases may be escalated when the SLA is at risk, while Sev-3 cases normally remain with standard support unless repeated failures or customer impact justify escalation.

## 4. Refund and Compensation
Refunds are considered when the customer was charged incorrectly, a service failure clearly caused a billable loss, or an approved compensation rule applies. Support must not promise a refund outside policy and should record the reason for any compensation decision.

## 5. Customer Communication Channels
Routine updates may be sent through the channel already used by the customer, such as the app or registered email. Sensitive account information must not be exposed through an unverified channel, and agents should ask the customer to use the approved account channel when identity is uncertain.

## 6. Business Hours and Holiday Support
Standard support operates during published business hours on working days. Critical Sev-1 operational incidents are monitored outside those hours, while routine requests received on holidays may be handled on the next working day.

## 7. Repeat Complaint Handling
A repeat complaint is linked to the earlier case so the customer does not need to restart the story. Repeated unresolved complaints may be escalated for specialist review when the original handling has failed to resolve the issue.

## 8. Service Credit
Service credits may be considered when an eligible service disruption meets the documented compensation criteria. Credits are not automatic and must not be promised until eligibility has been checked.

## 9. Feedback Collection
After a support interaction, customers may be invited to provide a short satisfaction rating and optional comments. Feedback is used to identify recurring problems and improve service quality, but it does not change an already approved policy decision by itself.

## 10. VIP Customer Handling
VIP customers receive the same policy decisions but may receive priority handling and a named escalation contact. VIP status does not allow staff to bypass security, verification, or mandatory approval controls.

## 11. Outage Communication
When a confirmed service outage affects multiple customers, support should communicate the incident status, known impact, and the next update expectation. Messages should avoid guessing about root cause or recovery time until the operations team has verified them.

## 12. Ticket Data Retention
Support-ticket information is retained only for the approved operational and compliance period. Access should follow least-privilege rules, and records should not be copied into unrelated systems when they are no longer needed.
